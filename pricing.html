Pricing Questions

Straight answers.

How do you decide which plan fits us?

On a quick consultation we look at your transaction volume, number of accounts, whether you need nonprofit fund accounting, and how much reporting you want. From there we recommend the plan that fits — and never up-sell you into more than you need.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?

For books that are current, onboarding is included. If your books need catch-up or clean-up first, we quote that as a separate one-time project so your ongoing monthly fee stays clean and predictable.

Are there long-term contracts?

No. We earn your business every month. Plans are month-to-month, and you can adjust as your organization grows or your needs change.

What if our needs change during the year?

Just tell us. We'll move you up or down a plan or adjust your custom scope so you're always paying for what you actually use.

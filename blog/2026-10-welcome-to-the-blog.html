From the Ledger

Welcome to the Mission Ledger Books blog

Oct 5, 2026 1 min read By the Mission Ledger Books team

Welcome, and thanks for stopping by. Once a month, we’ll share one practical idea to help you keep cleaner books and feel more in control of your numbers.

What you’ll find here

  • Month-end routines that keep your books current instead of piling up.
  • Nonprofit basics like restricted funds, grants, and board reporting.
  • Small-business tips for cash flow, categories, and reports you can actually read.
  • Straight answers to the questions we hear most often.

How we write

We are bookkeepers, not tax preparers, so you’ll see us point you to your CPA whenever a question moves into tax or legal territory. Everything here is meant to be useful on a busy Tuesday afternoon, not impressive in a textbook.

Got a question?

If there’s a bookkeeping topic you’d like us to cover, send us a note. Your question might become next month’s article.

General information only — not tax, legal, or accounting advice for your specific situation. Your CPA can confirm what applies to you.

Questions about your books? We’re happy to help.

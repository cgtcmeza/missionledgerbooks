What We Do

Full-service bookkeeping, tailored to you.

Pick the pieces you need or hand us the whole ledger. Every engagement is built around how your organization actually runs.

Monthly Bookkeeping

Every transaction categorized and every account reconciled, so your books close clean and on schedule — month after month.

Nonprofit Bookkeeping

Fund accounting, grant and restricted-fund tracking, and board-ready statements that keep your mission accountable and audit-ready.

Financial Reporting & Insights

Clear monthly P&L, balance sheet, and cash-flow reports — plus a plain-English read on what the numbers mean for your next decision.

Catch-Up & Clean-Up

Behind on the books? We'll untangle months (or years) of backlog and hand you a clean, reconciled foundation to build on.

Payables, Receivables & Payroll

Bills paid on time, invoices followed up, and payroll coordinated with your provider — the day-to-day money flow, handled.

Software Setup & Cleanup

QuickBooks and other major accounting systems set up right — chart of accounts, connections, and workflows — so your software works with you, not against you.
